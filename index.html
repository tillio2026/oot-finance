CSV Import Help — supported columns & tips

We auto-detect common column names. Most banks & cards work. Required: a date column and an amount column. Optional: description/memo, debit/credit columns.

Recognized headers: Date Posted Date Transaction Date · Amount Debit Credit · Description Memo Payee

Negative amounts are treated as expenses, positive as deposits. Some banks invert this — you can fix it during import.
